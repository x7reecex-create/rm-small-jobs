## Quote rules
### Ask every customer for
1. Postcode / area
2. Photos of the job and surrounding area
3. Exactly what they want done
4. Dimensions / number of items where relevant
5. For wall jobs: wall type if known
6. Whether they already have brackets, shelves or fixings
7. Preferred day/time

### Quote structure
Use a fixed job price wherever the scope is clear.

**Message format:**

> Cheers. Based on the photos/details, I can do that for £___. That covers the work we discussed. If anything is materially different when I arrive, I’ll tell you before doing any extra work or changing the price.

Do not quietly absorb major extra work and do not surprise the customer with a higher bill afterwards.

## Jobs to accept first
Prioritise jobs with low material cost, low transport burden and clear scope:
- Flat-pack desks, drawers, bedside units and simple beds
- Pictures, hooks and straightforward shelves on suitable walls
- Simple mirrors where weight/fixing requirements are understood
- Multiple small jobs in one nearby property
- Basic garden tidy-ups where the customer handles waste unless RM is properly authorised to transport it

## Jobs to be cautious with
- Large wardrobes or complex sliding-door systems
- Very heavy mirrors
- Large TVs or unusual brackets
- Plasterboard walls where a secure fixing method is uncertain
- Jobs above comfortable ladder height
- Anything where hidden cables/pipes cannot be assessed

## Do not take on
- Gas work
- Fixed electrical installation work
- Plumbing beyond very simple non-specialist tasks within proven competence
- Structural work
- Roofing / high-access work
- Asbestos-disturbing work
- Anything requiring a qualification, licence or experience RM does not have

## Wall-drilling rule
Before drilling, assess the wall and the possibility of hidden electrical cables, plumbing or other services. If the location cannot be assessed confidently, do not drill.

HSE guidance: https://www.hse.gov.uk/electricity/nearelectric.htm

## Insurance / admin
Public liability insurance is not generally the same as compulsory employers' liability insurance for a one-person sole trader, but government business guidance says businesses working with the public or visiting clients commonly need public liability cover. Get cover before scaling wall-mounting work.

HMRC: if total **gross trading income exceeds £1,000 in a tax year**, register for Self Assessment / sole trader reporting as required. Keep a simple record from job one: date, customer/job reference, money received, materials, travel and other business costs.

Sources:
- https://www.business.gov.uk/support/regulations-and-licensing/insuring-your-business/
- https://www.gov.uk/become-sole-trader/register-sole-trader

## Garden waste rule
Do not build the offer around taking customers' garden waste away until the relevant Scottish waste-transporter requirements have been checked and satisfied. The easy launch version is **tidy/cut/clear into the customer's own garden-waste bin or agreed pile/bags**.

SEPA register: https://search-the-register.sepa.org.uk/Find-Waste-Transporters-Brokers
