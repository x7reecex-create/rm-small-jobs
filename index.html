THE RM APPROACH

No pretending.
No risky jobs.

RM Small Jobs is deliberately straightforward. The aim is to do useful work well — not to say yes to everything.

01
Stay within my ability.
I won't take on work I can't confidently do properly.
02
Know the limits.
If it needs specialist knowledge, qualifications, equipment or insurance I don't have, I'll tell you.
03
Agree the job first.
We'll confirm what's being done and the price before work starts.
04
Leave it tidy.
Your home gets treated with respect.
